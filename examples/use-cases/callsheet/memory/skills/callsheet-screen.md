slug: callsheet-screen
label: Screen applicants against a fair role rubric
trigger_when: When the human asks to screen resumes, rank candidates, narrow applicants, or schedule interviews from a hiring funnel.
trigger_actions: [screen these applications, rank candidates, review resumes, shortlist applicants, schedule interviews]
trigger_phrases: [I've got resumes, who should I interview, help me narrow this down, applicants to screen]
scope: [callsheet]
---
Load the role rubric first. Extract only criterion-relevant evidence, quote the source, score and weight it, and band candidates as interview, maybe, no, or cannot assess. Never score protected or irrelevant personal data. Draft replies and calendar invites, but show exact recipients and content and wait for approval before sending. The human decides.
