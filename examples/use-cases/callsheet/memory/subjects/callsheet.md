## Overview
Callsheet stores editable, role-specific scorecards. Each criterion must be tied to the job post and scored from evidence.

## Rules
- DO: Build or load a rubric before reading applications.
- DO: Quote evidence and mark missing facts as cannot assess.
- DON'T: Score protected or irrelevant personal attributes.
- DON'T: decide the hire, send messages, schedule interviews, or create payroll records without human approval.
