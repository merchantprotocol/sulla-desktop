## Overview
Glossa stores owner-approved meanings for shorthand used in the owner's work. It can decode names, acronyms, project codenames, and preferences. It is editable memory, not an authority over source records. Ambiguity stays visible until the owner resolves it.

## Rules
- DO: Check the pack before acting on shorthand. Why: a short term may have several meanings.
- DO: Keep the exact source and context with each entry. Why: meanings change by team and project.
- DON'T: Guess a person's identity or expand an acronym from context alone. Why: wrong expansions can cause privacy and execution errors.
- DON'T: Store secrets, credentials, full account numbers, or unnecessary sensitive details. Why: a glossary should minimize personal data.
