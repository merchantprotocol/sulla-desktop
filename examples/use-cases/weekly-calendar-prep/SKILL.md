# Weekly calendar prep

Review the coming week and produce a practical preparation brief for a solo operator.

## When to use this skill

Use on Friday afternoon, Sunday evening, or whenever the owner asks to prepare for next week.

## Instructions

1. Read the next seven days from the connected calendar; do not create, move, or cancel events.
2. List meetings with missing agendas, external attendees, travel buffers, and conflicts.
3. Identify preparation tasks and group them by day, with realistic time estimates.
4. Call out deadlines and open blocks that could hold focused work.
5. Finish with a compact Monday-start checklist. Ask before writing anything back to the calendar.
