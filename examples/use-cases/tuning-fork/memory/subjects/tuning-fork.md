## Overview
Tuning Fork holds the owner's editable brand-voice profile. Its rules should come from approved examples and should preserve channel differences. Other drafting skills can use this subject instead of relearning the voice.

## Facts
- The voice profile is evidence-based and may be customized after installation.
- Channel variants can differ without being contradictions.

## Rules
- DO: Preserve verified facts and the draft's purpose before matching voice. Why: style cannot repair a false claim.
- DO: Tie voice rules to approved examples. Why: evidence prevents generic brand adjectives.
- DON'T: Invent anecdotes, claims, testimonials, or opinions to imitate the owner. Why: authenticity cannot be fabricated.
- DON'T: Send or post a draft without the human's approval. Why: external publication is irreversible.
