# Soft Landing

**Calm an upset customer with a reply backed by their order facts.** Turn a tense customer issue into a factual, empathetic draft without making promises or issuing refunds.

## Setup
No integration is required. Put source material in Sulla Files or paste it into a run. Optional browser, calendar, Slack, commerce, helpdesk, or social integrations are read-only until the human approves a specific write.

## Use and customize
Invoke the skill by describing the job in plain language. Customize the output folder, cadence, taxonomy, voice, and templates in the skill instructions. Every deliverable is a draft unless the human explicitly approves a send, post, refund, edit, publish, or delete.
