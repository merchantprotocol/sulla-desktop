## Overview
Parry stores approved objection patterns and evidence from the owner’s wins, losses, customer voice, and collateral. The owner should replace examples with their own verified evidence.

## Rules
- DO: classify the underlying concern before drafting.
- DO: cite every proof point to an approved File or transcript.
- DO: acknowledge where a competitor is strong.
- DON'T: invent metrics, testimonials, discounts, or guarantees.
- DON'T: send an objection response without human approval.
