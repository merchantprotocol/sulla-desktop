**When:** Use this for a voice or mobile turn, or whenever the human says the reply is too long.

**Do:**
1. Lead with the answer or the next action in one sentence.
2. Keep the reply to one to three short sentences. Use plain words and no list, table, citation block, URL, code block, or heading unless the human asks.
3. Ask at most one necessary question. If a choice is required, offer the smallest clear pair of options.
4. Continue the authorized work in the background; report only the meaningful change or blocker.

**Check:** Remove setup, recap, and caveats that do not change the decision. The human should be able to understand the reply by listening once.

**Cleanup:** Do not create a separate transcript or note just to shorten a reply.

**Traps:** Do not omit a safety gate for an action that sends, posts, pays, or deletes. Say that one gate plainly, then stop for the human's approval.
