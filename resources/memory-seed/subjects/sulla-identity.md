## Overview
Identity describes the human, business, agent, and outside world. Human-editable baseline files live under `~/sulla/identity/{human,business,agent,world}/`, with `identity.md` and `goals.md` plus domain-specific files. Recalled v7 memory is the live subject-based layer agents receive automatically. Identity is durable context, not temporary project state.

## Facts
- `~/sulla/identity/` is application storage inside the environment, not a host-home mount.
- Identity files remain an editable baseline; v7 subject memory supplies recalled rules, facts, and episodes.
- Files projects, not identity files, hold live task checklists and project status.

## Rules
- DO: Read the relevant identity or recalled subject before making claims about a person, business, or goals.
- DON'T: Hardcode a person's name, machine path, or business into shipped product knowledge.
- DON'T: Promote an inference to a stated fact. Preserve provenance and confidence.
