# Contributing to Sulla Desktop

Sulla Desktop is licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later. Preserve third-party copyright notices, add an `SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-2.0-or-later` header to new Merchant Protocol source files, and run `node scripts/check-license-headers.mjs` before opening a pull request.

## Contributor License Agreement

Outside contributions require acceptance of the [Merchant Protocol LLC Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA grants Merchant Protocol LLC the rights needed to use contributions in both GPL-licensed Sulla Desktop and separately licensed products such as proprietary Sulla Server, while contributors retain ownership of their work.

The CLA and its automated check are drafts and are not yet in effect. Do not collect signatures until Merchant Protocol LLC publishes the approved text and acceptance process.
